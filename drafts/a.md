LEAD

Binance obtains a $6.5 billion freezing order after its theft.

An Abu Dhabi court has given Binance a legal tool to pursue assets taken in the October 29 attack, the exchange told The Block. The order's ceiling is not a measure of money recovered. [https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-18-binance-freezing-court-order-adgm-crypto-october-hack-443491]

The Abu Dhabi Global Market Courts issued a worldwide freezing order against unidentified alleged attackers, Binance said in a statement reported by The Block's Yogita Khatri on November 18. Justice William Stone issued the order, according to the exchange. The injunction covers up to $6.5 billion, against approximately $6.34 billion in assets affected by the attack. [https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-18-binance-freezing-court-order-adgm-crypto-october-hack-443491]

Binance had previously said roughly $2.85 billion had been frozen or intercepted. That figure is smaller than the injunction's ceiling, and neither figure establishes how much has been returned to the exchange. Binance also obtained interim orders concerning specific assets and orders for assets to be handed over, the company told The Block. [https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-18-binance-freezing-court-order-adgm-crypto-october-hack-443491]

Some named defendants had already frozen stolen assets, and Binance does not accuse those parties of wrongdoing, the report said. The exchange said some issuers, exchanges and other partners require a court order before releasing or returning frozen funds. Binance said the orders can be served through a blockchain. [https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-18-binance-freezing-court-order-adgm-crypto-october-hack-443491]

Binance's lawyers identified 26,000 wallet addresses that they say hold stolen assets, and the exchange said the list will change as funds move. Elliptic co-founder Tom Robinson previously told The Block that issuers would want a clear legal basis before reissuing frozen stablecoins. He also estimated that about $3.3 billion remained in bitcoin, ether and XRP, assets he said nobody could freeze. [https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-18-binance-freezing-court-order-adgm-crypto-october-hack-443491]

The published account does not establish how much the order will ultimately recover. The distinction matters to customers waiting for a durable resolution and to partners holding frozen assets. The court order itself was not independently located for this edition. [https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-18-binance-freezing-court-order-adgm-crypto-october-hack-443491]

NEWS

[NEWS] An Ethereum savings vault loses about $3.42 million.

On November 18, K3 Capital's sBOLD vault lost nearly all of its holdings in eight minutes, according to blockchain investigator Bitquery. Bitquery traced 97 drain transactions from six wallets and estimated that block builders received $1.78 million. Its analysis disputes an early account that the attack involved loan liquidations; the firm also says it did not establish whether an earlier audited fix was deployed. [https://bitquery.io/investigations/k3-capital-sbold-hack]

[NEWS] Ledger buyers report losses in Dubai and Berlin.

Bitquery traced $1.68 million in losses across 16 wallets on November 14 and 16, after three Ledger owners reported thefts. It says a TRON address linked to an earlier theft paid fees for two of the newly drained wallets. The investigators could not inspect the devices and say the chain alone cannot establish how the thief obtained the recovery phrases. [https://bitquery.io/investigations/ledger-hack-dubai-berlin]

[NEWS] The World Bank explores stablecoin payments with Citi and Circle.

On November 17, the World Bank Group said it would examine dollar stablecoins and blockchain payment infrastructure with Citi and Circle. The work will consider how these payments might complement its existing correspondent banking arrangements. The announcement describes exploration, and does not announce a live rollout of stablecoin disbursements. [https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/11/17/world-bank-group-explores-regulated-stablecoins-to-modernize-payments-redirect-res]

TECH

[TECH] Researchers measure the network cost of aggregated proofs.

On November 18, masthead researcher Csaba Kiraly published simulations of networking choices for Ethereum proposal EIP-8288. At 15 signature transactions per second, pushing proofs to six peers used 11.9 to 13.3 megabits per second per node in the modeled networks. The paper proposes limiting push peers and specifying when nodes fetch proofs; the findings are simulations, not a deployment result. [https://ethresear.ch/t/eip-8288-the-networking-side-of-recursive-aggregation/26263]

[TECH] A wallet prototype tests an encrypted PIN on Ethereum.

On November 18, developer Himess described a smart account that checks an encrypted PIN and counts guesses on-chain. The author says the design depends on Zama's threshold network and identifies both collusion and denial-of-service risks. The account is an unaudited research prototype on the Sepolia test network, not a production security guarantee. [https://ethresear.ch/t/encrypted-account-a-pin-that-lives-in-the-contract/26265]

CULTURE

[CULTURE] Aave Labs challenges the UK's draft DeFi boundary.

In a November 18 response to the Financial Conduct Authority, Aave Labs argued that the regulator's two consultations pull in different directions. The company said maintenance, screening and risk controls should not by themselves make a protocol participant a regulated intermediary. Its letter presents an interested industry position; it does not establish the regulator's final interpretation. [https://aave.com/images/cms/aave-labs-fca-defi-perimeter.pdf]

[CULTURE] Aave contributors propose adding USDC on Plasma.

On November 18, governance contributor TokenLogic proposed listing native USDC on Aave V3's Plasma market. The proposal says USDT0 is the market's only listed fiat-backed stablecoin and argues another issuer would reduce concentration. Risk providers still have to recommend final parameters, and the proposal is not evidence that the listing has passed. [https://governance.aave.com/t/direct-to-aip-asset-listing-usdc-plasma/26438]
